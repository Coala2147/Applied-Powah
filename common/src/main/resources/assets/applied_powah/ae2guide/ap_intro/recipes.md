# Recipes

## Cells

| Result | Ingredients |
|--------|-------------|
| Super dense energy cell | 8× `ae2:energy_cell` + `ae2:calculation_processor` |
| Extreme dense energy cell | 8× super dense + `ae2:calculation_processor` |

## Rods from Powah rods

`a` = AE2 energy cell; `b` = same-tier Powah energizing rod.

**AE** (cross): `_ a _ / a b a / _ a _`

**ME** (corners): `a _ a / _ b _ / a _ a`

## Upgrades

Same materials as the Powah upgrade for the next tier; the rod slot uses the previous-tier Applied Powah rod (AE or ME family).

## AE ↔ ME

Shapeless conversion both ways, same tier.

## Gating

| Mods | Available |
|------|-----------|
| AE2 + Powah + Flux | All cells and rods |
| AE2 + Powah | Cells and AE rods |
| AE2 only | Cells only |

## JEI

With JEI, Applied Powah rods appear as catalysts on Powah's Energizing category. Crafted recipes remain workbench recipes.
