# Upgrade cards

Every orb has **5** upgrade slots on the right of its GUI, taking AE2's own
cards — Applied Powah ships no custom cards:

| Card | Fluix | Advanced / ME | Effect |
|------|-------|---------------|--------|
| <ItemLink id="ae2:speed_card" /> | ×5 | ×5 | Acceleration: each page's reaction runs faster (spreads across the machine's pages) |
| <ItemLink id="ae2:energy_card" /> | — | ×4 | Bigger energy cache and faster network IO (Advanced / ME tiers only) |

Up to **5 speed cards** and up to **4 energy cards** can be installed at once.
The tier decides which cards are accepted — family caps are enforced on insert.

Advanced and ME orbs carry 4 independent recipe pages; extra pages are physical
slots of the block, not cards.
